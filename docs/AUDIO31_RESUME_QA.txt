Pinned SDL_mixer investigation
The project pins SDL_mixer 2.8.1 (commit 171eb2d420d5643e4ee11514a06e04a41a463bbd).
Android CMake enables MP3 through minimp3, not mpg123. SDL_mixer.h declares
Mix_GetMusicPosition, Mix_MusicDuration, Mix_SetMusicPosition and
Mix_FadeInMusicPos. music.c permits Mix_SetMusicPosition only after a music
object starts; Mix_FadeInMusicPos starts and seeks under the audio lock before
playback becomes active. The latter is used for resume with an incoming fade.
Music objects are opened once per deck visit (Combat on demand) and retained
across state switches; the AUDIO3 state machine waits for outgoing fade
completion before starting the next object.

minimp3's Tell reads dec.cur_sample / (channels * sample_rate): decoded PCM
sample time, not encoded MP3 byte offset. Its duration uses decoded sample
count. Its seek uses MP3D_SEEK_TO_SAMPLE and a frame index with predecode for
the MP3 bit reservoir. AudioStream buffering means reported position can be
slightly ahead of audio at the output. The minimp3 Seek wrapper returns 0
without propagating mp3dec_ex_seek's result; a successful SDL_mixer return
therefore does not alone prove the decoder reached the requested sample.
The immediately reported timestamp is logged for comparison, but an Android
listening test is needed to establish audible accuracy and seek latency.

Runtime behavior
For the active deck visit, Explore and Combat each have started, valid and
position fields. A first play starts at 0. A transition captures the outgoing
position before Mix_FadeOutMusic. Invalid (negative, NaN, over 24 hours, or
more than one second beyond a known duration) readings are discarded. Once
the asynchronous fade ends, a returning track starts through
Mix_FadeInMusicPos at its saved position. If that call fails, the track is
retried at 0 and the seek failure is logged; ordinary playback failure keeps
AUDIO3's Combat-to-Explore or Explore-to-stock fallback. A retarget during an
outgoing fade resumes the final requested track using its own memory.

Temporary near-end rule: when duration is known and fewer than five seconds
remain at the saved position, restart at 0. For unknown duration, do not
guess a safe endpoint. This rule is deliberately simple and will be replaced
by per-track loop/resume regions in a future milestone. Natural full-file
looping still restarts abruptly at the file beginning.

Deck changes discard both positions. musicai_clear, invoked during game
closedown/new game/load, shuts down retained external decoders and resets
runtime positions and threat state, including same-deck loads. Nothing is
serialized. Theme/menu suspension pauses the current decoder and pending
fade without clearing positions; SDL device background pause remains unchanged.
Audiolog ducking only changes volume. Missing Combat keeps Explore; missing
Explore restores stock. No fake position is stored for an absent Combat file.

Diagnostics
AUDIO3.1 logs one save per outgoing transition, requested resume, immediately
reported position and delta, invalid reading, near-end guard, seek failure,
runtime reset, suspend and theme/lifecycle resume. It does not poll/log every
frame. Compare requested and reported positions with listening: minimp3's
decoded cursor may be ahead of the audible output.

   listen 15-20 seconds, then end combat. Confirm Explore resumes near where

5. Teleport to another deck. Confirm its first Explore and Combat plays begin
